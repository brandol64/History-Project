19th Century Reform Movements

During the 1800s many reformers worked to improve American society. They fought to end slavery, expand women's rights, improve public education, reform prisons and mental hospitals, and create a more equal society.

Frederick Douglass

Formerly enslaved abolitionist who became one of the most powerful voices against slavery. His speeches and writings helped convince many Americans to support abolition.
